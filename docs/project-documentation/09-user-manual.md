# 9. User Manual
## Shopper
1. Open the web application and browse categories or the product catalogue.
2. Search for a product and apply available filters.
3. Open product details and add desired items to the cart.
4. Register/sign in to save account-specific data.
5. Maintain wishlist and preferences such as dietary choices, allergens to avoid, favourite brands, and budget.
6. Request recommendations or substitutions and review the reasons/data.
7. Explore recipes and meal plans; check ingredients, pack quantities, and estimated costs.
8. Use basket optimisation to review possible savings, duplicates, expensive lines, or preference conflicts. Suggestions require shopper approval.
9. Review cart, choose/provide address, and submit using the available mock/COD demonstration mode.
10. Review order confirmation and order history; cancellation depends on order-state rules.

## Guest cart
A guest cart is associated with a guest identifier. After login, the application supports merging the guest cart into the account cart.

## Administrator
1. Sign in with an administrator account.
2. Open the admin area and inspect dashboard statistics.
3. Create/update/delete products as permitted.
4. Update stock and maintain categories.
5. Review orders and update permitted order states.

## Accuracy and safety
Nutrition/allergen details depend on catalogue quality. Recommendations are not medical advice. Substitutes are not guaranteed nutritionally equivalent. Prices and stock are application catalogue values, not guaranteed external market data. Do not enter real card details into the mock payment workflow.
